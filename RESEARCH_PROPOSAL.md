# AI Safety and Alignment Research Proposal

## Research Title
Improving the Safety, Reasoning, and Reliability of Large Language Models.

## Research Objectives
- Reduce AI hallucinations.
- Improve reasoning accuracy.
- Evaluate AI safety and alignment.
- Investigate uncertainty awareness.
- Explore AI self-correction techniques.

## Research Questions
- Can AI models identify and correct their own mistakes?
- Can structured verification reduce hallucinations?
- How reliably do AI models respond to misleading information?

## Methodology
- Develop evaluation datasets.
- Conduct experiments using Claude models.
- Compare different prompting and safety techniques.
- Measure accuracy, hallucination rates, and reliability.
- Document experimental results and limitations.

## Required Resources
- Anthropic API credits
- Python
- Open-source evaluation datasets
- GitHub

## Expected Outcomes
A publicly documented evaluation framework, experimental findings, and recommendations for improving AI safety and reliability.

## Current Status
Research planning and preparation. Experiments have not yet been conducted.
