# AI Safety and Alignment Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Research Foundations
- Study AI safety and alignment.
- Review existing research papers.
- Identify limitations of large language models.

## Phase 2 — Infrastructure Development
- Set up the Python research environment.
- Integrate the Anthropic API.
- Develop an automated evaluation framework.

## Phase 3 — Dataset Development
- Prepare hallucination evaluation datasets.
- Create reasoning benchmarks.
- Develop uncertainty and reliability tests.

## Phase 4 — Experimental Research
- Evaluate Claude models.
- Investigate hallucination reduction techniques.
- Test self-correction mechanisms.
- Compare different prompting strategies.
- Evaluate model alignment and reliability.

## Phase 5 — Results and Analysis
- Analyze experimental results.
- Compare model performance.
- Identify limitations and failure patterns.
- Document reproducible findings.

## Phase 6 — Research Publication
- Publish research code on GitHub.
- Release evaluation datasets.
- Publish a research report.
- Share recommendations with the AI safety community.

## Phase 7 — Continuous Improvement
- Incorporate research feedback.
- Improve evaluation techniques.
- Explore additional AI safety methods.

## Current Status
Research planning and infrastructure preparation. Experimental research has not yet begun.
